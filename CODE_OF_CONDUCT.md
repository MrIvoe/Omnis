# Code of Conduct

Omnis is a hobby project that's starting to take on outside
contributors and plugin authors. This document says what "be
respectful and assume good faith" (already the one line in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)) means in practice, so it doesn't
have to be improvised issue by issue.

## The standard

- Disagree about code, architecture, or design as much as you want —
  that's normal and welcome. Keep it about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith before assuming bad faith. Most confusing PRs and
  blunt-sounding issues are exactly that: confusing or blunt, not
  hostile.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes,
  unwelcome sexual attention, or doxxing, in any project space (issues,
  PRs, discussions, or anywhere else someone is representing or
  engaging with the project).
- Respect that this is maintained on someone's personal time. A slow
  response or a "not right now" to a feature isn't a snub.

## Scope

This applies to the Omnis and Omnis-Plugins repositories — issues,
pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and any other space
where someone is participating in or representing the project.

## Reporting

If something needs attention that a public issue isn't the right place
for, contact the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile
([@MrIvoe](https://github.com/MrIvoe)). Reports are handled privately,
and the identity of anyone reporting in good faith is kept
confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what response fits a given situation — a
private word, a public warning, or removal from the project's spaces
(blocked from the repo, comments removed, PR closed). Repeated or
severe violations lead to a permanent block. There's no formal appeals
process at this project's current size; if you think a decision was
wrong, say so directly and it'll be reconsidered on the merits.

This document is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), trimmed
to fit a small project rather than copied wholesale.
